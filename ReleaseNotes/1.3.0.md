WattBar 1.3.0 · macOS 13+ · Apple silicon and Intel

- A redesigned menu with live power history, average draw, peak draw, and recorded energy.
- A complete Energy & Apps dashboard with Overview, Applications, and History pages, interactive charts, and application search and sorting.
- Rolling energy summaries for the past minute, hour, day, week, month, and three months (7, 30, and 90 days).
- Per-app CPU energy on supported Macs, with CPU time where energy counters are unavailable. App details include exited applications and optional background processes.
- Up to 90 days of local history, with recent detail and compact hourly summaries for older measurements. Existing saved history migrates automatically.
- Native macOS Preferences with General, Menu Bar, Monitoring, and Updates tabs, a live menu preview, and clearer setting descriptions.
- A new mint-and-charcoal app icon throughout the app.

Averages use recorded time. Sleep, missing readings, and time before WattBar was running remain gaps; longer periods fill as readings accumulate. Previously expired history cannot be recovered. Per-app CPU energy does not include display, GPU, network, or other device power and is not a breakdown of whole-Mac consumption.

Validation: 52 core tests, 11 release metadata tests, a universal Apple silicon/Intel build, and Sparkle signature and tamper-rejection smoke checks passed. The dashboard, Preferences, and saved-history restoration were checked on an Apple silicon desktop Mac. MacBook battery/charger transitions, physical sleep/wake, login-item approval, and Intel hardware still need testing on those setups. Sensor availability varies by model.
